# Android Notices

There are two builds: ECC-P384, which uses a digital signature scheme based on the ECC-P384 algorithm, and RSA-4096, which uses a signature scheme based on the 4096-bit RSA algorithm.

**These two versions cannot be installed over one another!** Please choose your preferred version. For better security, use ECC-P384. For better compatibility, use 4096-bit RSA.
